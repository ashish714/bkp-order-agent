# BKP Customer FAQ

## Shipping
- **How long does delivery take?** Metro cities: 2-4 business days. Other locations: 4-7 business days.
- **Is shipping free?** Yes, on orders of ₹999 or more. Below that, a flat ₹49 shipping fee applies.
- **Do you ship outside India?** Not yet. BKP currently ships within India only.
- **Can I change my delivery address?** Only while the order is still PROCESSING (before it ships). Contact support with your order ID.

## Tracking
- **How do I track my order?** Share your order ID (format BKP-XXXXX) and the email used to place it, and we'll give you the latest status. Once shipped, you also get the carrier's tracking number.
- **My order says delivered but I didn't get it.** Check with neighbours or your building's security first. If it's still missing after 24 hours, contact support and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.

## Returns & refunds
- **What is the return window?** 30 days from delivery for most items, as long as they're unused and in original packaging.
- **Which orders can be refunded?**
  - DELIVERED orders within 30 days of delivery: full refund of item price (shipping fee is non-refundable).
  - PROCESSING orders (not yet shipped): can be cancelled for a full refund including shipping.
  - SHIPPED / IN_TRANSIT orders: cannot be refunded until delivered; you can refuse delivery or request a return after it arrives.
  - CANCELLED orders: already refunded automatically.
- **How long do refunds take?** UPI: 1-3 business days. Cards: 5-7 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- **Do I need to send the item back?** For delivered orders, yes. A pickup is scheduled within 2 business days of the refund request.

## Payments
- **Which payment methods do you accept?** UPI, credit cards, debit cards and net banking. Cash on delivery is available for orders under ₹5,000.
- **Is my payment information safe?** Yes. BKP never stores your full card number.

## Contact
- **How do I reach a human?** Email support@bkp.example or call 1800-000-0000 (Mon-Sat, 9am-7pm IST).
